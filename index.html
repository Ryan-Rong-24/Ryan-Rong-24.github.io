Ryan Rong

I'm a freshman studying CS at Stanford University. I co-lead the Machine Learning Lab at Stanford ACM where we host school-wide events like the Puzzle Hunt. I'm also a student researcher at Stanford AI Lab interested in language models, 3D vision, and its intersection in medicine. Outside of academics, I enjoy performing in musicals with Stanford Ram's Head.

In high school, I competed in computing olympiads like USACO (Platinum), USA Biology Olympiad. I also competed in FIRST Robotics (FRC) on team 5895 Peddie Robotics.
